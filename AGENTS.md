# Agent Guidance

## Commit attribution

- Attribute every commit solely to the human who requested the work.
- Never use Amp or any other AI/agent identity as a commit author or coauthor, and never add an AI `Co-authored-by` trailer.
- Before committing, if Git's configured identity is missing or belongs to an AI/agent, supply the human requester's identity explicitly. Ask the requester if their identity is not known.
